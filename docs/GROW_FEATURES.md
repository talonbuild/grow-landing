# GROW — Features & User Flow (source of truth for site copy)

GROW is a daily consistency app built around a personal action plan called a **Brainwave**. Users choose repeatable actions, commit to them, and return each day to follow through. An evolving brain represents their progress across three areas: **Physical, Mental, and Life**.

The central loop is **Prime → Act → Reset → Reflect**. Completing even one action counts as showing up; completing every action in a sector makes that sector grow.

## The app's main features

GROW has four main destinations: **Grow, Brainwaves, Reflect, and Settings**. Progress is accessed from the Grow dashboard.

- **Grow — the daily dashboard.** An interactive brain displays the user's three sectors and their current state. Users tap into a sector to see and complete its daily actions, record how an action felt, or write a reflection. The dashboard also provides the Showing Up counter, morning/evening rituals, and access to Progress. Brain animations and completion celebrations make progress visible.
- **Brainwaves — the user's daily commitment.** Users build a plan from scratch or customize a curated starting plan. They choose one or more sectors, select categories, add their own actions, name the plan, review it, and "seal" it. A Brainwave supports up to ten actions per sector. Physical covers areas such as strength, cardio, and nutrition; Mental includes focus, calm, and reflection; Life includes money, relationships, and managing one's environment.
- **PRIME and RESET — short daily rituals.** PRIME helps users prepare their environment and reduce friction before acting. RESET helps them settle their environment and prepare for tomorrow. These are guided checklists with their own visual completion feedback. They support the routine but do not replace action completion or earn sector growth.
- **Reflect — journaling connected to the routine.** Users can record emotions and write reflections about individual actions, the overall Brainwave cycle, or anything else on their mind. Entries can be revisited, edited, and deleted. The overall Brainwave reflection is limited to one per cycle; reflection is optional.
- **Progress — evidence of consistency.** Users can inspect current and best Showing Up records, sector Growing records, Lock-In streaks, total completed cycles, recent activity, category distribution, and earned milestones called Frequencies. Each sector develops independently, so progress shows where effort is going.
- **Settings and personalization.** Light/dark appearance, English/Spanish, account and subscription screens, reminder controls, support and feedback flows, and privacy/data controls. Daily reminders and ritual reminders have separate switches.

## How progress works

GROW separates participation, growth, and full completion:

| Measure | What earns it |
|---|---|
| **Showing Up** | Complete at least one action anywhere in the Brainwave during a cycle. |
| **Sector Growing** | Complete every action in a particular sector. Partial completion preserves its existing Growing count without advancing it. |
| **Whole Brainwave Lock-In** | Complete every action across all active sectors during the cycle. |

A GROW cycle runs from **7:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. the following day**. PRIME is available from 7:00 a.m.; RESET becomes available at 7:00 p.m. At the next 7:00 a.m. boundary, the completed cycle becomes history and the daily checklist renews.

Missing a cycle can put an established Showing Up streak into **Dormant** status, giving the user one cycle to reconnect. Returning with one action preserves and advances that streak. Missing the grace cycle resets the current streak, while recorded history remains. Sectors have their own recovery logic: doing something in a dormant sector preserves its growth; completing everything advances it. Consecutive Lock-In streaks have no grace period.

Sector growth unlocks nine **Frequencies**, at 3, 5, 10, 14, 21, 30, 45, 60, and 90 Growing days. These provide milestones and opportunities to adjust the plan.

## How users change their plan

A sealed Brainwave is a commitment with deliberate editing options:

- **Upgrade:** A newly earned Frequency unlocks a full editing opportunity.
- **Rewire:** Spend one Rewire to edit the existing Brainwave. Users initially receive three; qualifying milestones refresh the balance to three. A Rewire is spent only when the edited plan is sealed.
- **Break:** End the current Brainwave and build a replacement. Current streaks reset, but historical records and earned achievements remain.

Upgrade and Rewire changes take effect at the **next cycle**, keeping the current day's commitments stable.

## The first-time user flow

1. Welcome and choose focus areas. New users can start as guests; returning users have a login route.
2. Complete an assessment. Questions explore goals, consistency patterns, distractions, follow-through, and recovery after missed days. These responses shape the onboarding presentation rather than changing progression rules.
3. Learn the system. Feature slides explain Brainwaves, sectors, actions, rituals, and progress.
4. Build and seal the first Brainwave through a seal ceremony.
5. Receive a plan keepsake (exportable image) and choose membership (yearly with free trial, or monthly).
6. Complete the guided tutorial (practice data).
7. Enter the daily dashboard.

## The returning daily flow

Open Grow → complete PRIME → enter a brain sector → check off actions → receive Showing Up and sector-growth feedback → optionally record emotions or reflections → complete RESET in the evening → return for the next cycle.
