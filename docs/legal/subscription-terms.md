# Subscription Terms

*Last updated 19 September 2026*

What you are paying for, when you are charged, and how to stop.

## What a subscription gives you

A GROW subscription unlocks the whole app: your Brainwave, the daily loop, your living brain and progress, the Prime and Reset rituals, reflections and everything that comes with them.

## The plans

The exact price, in your own currency, is shown on the plan screen before you buy, and it is the price that applies. Prices differ between countries and can change over time.

- Yearly — billed once a year, and it begins with a free trial.
- Monthly — billed every month, with no trial.

## The free trial

The yearly plan starts with a free trial of the length shown on the plan screen. You are not charged during the trial.

Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it turns into a paid year automatically and your Apple ID is charged.

A trial is once per person. If you have had one before, or you already have a subscription, any unused part of it is forfeited when you subscribe.

## Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically. Your Apple ID is charged within 24 hours before the current period ends, and the subscription continues for another period of the same length at the price then in effect.

It keeps renewing until you cancel. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the period ends stops the next charge.

## How to cancel

Cancel in your Apple account, not in GROW — we cannot cancel it for you: open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose GROW, then Cancel Subscription. You can also reach the same page from GROW under Settings → Manage subscriptions.

When you cancel you keep everything you paid for until the end of the period you already paid for. Nothing is lost from your account when it ends — your Brainwave and your history are still there if you come back.

## Refunds

Apple handles all payments and therefore all refunds. We cannot issue one. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where the law where you live gives you a right to withdraw or a refund, that right stands regardless of anything here.

## Price changes

If we change the price of a plan, the new price applies from your next renewal. Apple will tell you in advance, and where Apple requires your agreement, your subscription will not renew at the new price until you give it.

## Getting a subscription back

If you reinstall GROW, change phone, or sign in somewhere else, tap Restore Purchases on the plan screen or in Settings → Manage subscriptions. Your subscription belongs to your account and to the Apple ID that bought it.

## Family Sharing

GROW subscriptions are not shared through Apple Family Sharing.

## The rest

These Subscription Terms are part of the Terms of Use, and the Privacy Policy explains what we hold about your subscription. If something here conflicts with Apple's own terms for a purchase you made through the App Store, Apple's terms govern that purchase.

Questions about a payment: support@talonapp.co.
